Privacy Policy (App)

Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Masterlist: Focus & Tasks, available on the Apple App Store, made by Lone Knight LLC, Texas, USA.

App Store page: apps.apple.com/us/app/masterlist-focus-tasks

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@masterlistapp.com.

The short version

Everything you create in the app — tasks, notes, logs, statistics — stays on your device, and, if you turn on backups to iCloud Drive, in your own iCloud account. We never receive or store it. Apart from those backups, what leaves your device is limited to usage statistics and purchase records, each handled by the providers below. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, usage statistics are shared only if you allow them.

What stays on your device

All your tasks, notes, logs, statistics, and similar content are stored locally and never sent to us. We have no server-side copy, no account system, and no way to read what you enter.

Backups to iCloud Drive. If you turn this on, the app saves copies of its automatic backups to your own iCloud Drive, where Apple stores them in your iCloud account under its terms. We don't receive them. You can turn this off in the app's menu and delete the copies in the Files app.

What is collected, and by whom

Usage statistics. Google Analytics for Firebase records how the app is used: for example, which screens and features are used, how long focus sessions are, in-app purchases, and whether errors occur. Each installation gets a random ID; the app doesn't use your device's advertising identifier, and never sends your name, the content you create, or anything else that identifies you by name. Google also receives technical information such as the device model, iOS version, language, and an approximate location derived from the IP address.

Apple. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple gives us aggregated usage figures and crash reports.

Purchases. Purchases and donations are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat checks your Pro status for us: it receives the records of your purchases in Masterlist (never other apps) and a random ID created by the app, not your name or Apple Account.

Because these services run inside the app, their handling of data is also governed by their own policies, including Google's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. Each of them protects your data at least as well as this policy describes. We don't sell your data.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If your device's region is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Masterlist asks you once whether to share usage statistics. Nothing is collected until you allow it, and you can change your answer anytime in the app's menu.

Legal bases: usage statistics, your consent; purchases, providing what you bought.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • We don't keep any of your data on our own systems.
  • Google keeps detailed usage statistics for 2 months, and information tied to an installation's random ID for 14 months after the app was last used, as set in the app's Firebase settings; then it deletes them automatically. Overall reports, and anything else Google keeps, are covered by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own privacy policies: Apple's and RevenueCat's.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device, including the random IDs it created. If you install it again, it gets new IDs, so new usage statistics can't be linked to the old ones.
  • Backups you saved to iCloud Drive stay there until you delete them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete data about you, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting what happened before. Since we hold none of your content ourselves, most requests will point to the providers above, but you're welcome to reach us at support@masterlistapp.com and we'll help. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time, effective on publication.
